## Gaps that name SOSA/SSN (model's summaries)

- `s2orc:289217324` Semantic modelling of AI-integrated digital twins for enhanced transparency: SOSA/SSN models individual observations and actuations but not continuous sensing or actuation processes and streams, so new activity classes and a DataStream class are introduced. Reusing sosa:observedProperty, sosa:actsOnProperty, sosa:madeBySensor or sosa:madeByActuator would infer activities as sosa:Observation or sosa:Actuation, so dedicated properties are defined instead. The ssn-system classes are redefined in ADO, the authors believing the Systems module belongs only to an earlier specification. A link from continuous activities to individual observations via EP-PLAN is proposed as future work.
- `openalex:W7149348416` Interoperability and Upgradability Improvement for Context-Aware Systems in Agriculture 4.0: sosa:Observation does not distinguish measurement, aggregation and deduction, and cannot describe the inputs of an aggregation (addressed with caso:Observation and prov:wasInformedBy); there is no vocabulary for qualitative data or states (caso:State, caso:Boundary added); the use of domainIncludes/rangeIncludes causes OOPS! pitfalls P10, P11 and P13; domain-specific devices, properties, units and actions are not covered; the cbar unit is missing from unit ontologies; the link from deductions to actuator commands is still open
- `openalex:W4297147174` Semantic Web Technologies for Indoor Environmental Quality: A Review and Ontology Design: SSN adds owl:allValuesFrom and minCardinality restrictions that are stricter than SOSA's schema:domainIncludes/rangeIncludes. ssn:isPropertyOf is not functional, which conflicts with the intrinsic nature of properties (reported from the AffectedBy ODP). The sensor-specific SOSA pattern was generalised to Executor/Execution (EEP, BOP) to cover static and dynamic properties. No alignment of BOP to SOSA was made, for fear of too-strong ontological commitments. Some extensions (UDSA, WISDOM) use outdated SSN versions.
